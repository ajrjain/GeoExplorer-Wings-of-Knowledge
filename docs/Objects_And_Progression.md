# Objects and Progression

## Unlockable Planes (Player Vehicles)
1. **Propeller Plane (Default)**: Balanced speed, classic look.
2. **Jet Fighter**: High speed, agile turning. Unlocked after 5 regions.
3. **Glider**: Silent, slow speed (great for exploring and sightseeing). Unlocked after 1000 points.

## Points System
*   **Base Discovery**: 100 Points per landmark collected.
*   **Completion Bonus**: 500 Points for finding all landmarks in a region.
*   **Weather Bonus**: +50 points per landmark if found during Stormy weather.

## In-Game Collectibles (Objects)
*   **Golden Compasses**: Restores "boost" meter (future feature).
*   **Cloud Rings**: Flying through them gives a temporary speed boost.
